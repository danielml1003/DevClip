        Enable LAN sync?

        This starts a local network service on this device so you can sync with other DevClip machines. Before you turn it on:

        • Your device becomes discoverable to anything on your local network (Wi-Fi / LAN).
        • While sync is on, any DevClip on your network can connect and receive all your snippets and your entire clipboard history — which may include passwords, tokens and other secrets. There is no per-device approval.
        • Only enable this on networks you trust, and turn it off when you're done.
